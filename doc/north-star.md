# North star — Personal Portfolio

Reviewed with the owner every 5 phases. Keep it to one page.

## Who it is for

1. Freelance clients, first: a technical stranger deciding whether to hire the owner for a build.
2. Hiring managers checking that the owner ships production systems.
3. Anyone who needs a current, presentable resume.

## The problem

1. A stranger cannot judge a product engineer from a list of skills; they need to see real work and
   how it was built, and most of that work sits in private repos.
2. The order of the three audiences must be changeable without rewriting the site.

## What success looks like

1. A technical stranger who lands on `/` gets in touch or books a call (the site's one job).
2. The ETP case study and the delivery-pipeline page show depth without any private repo.
3. Every page scores 95 or more on Lighthouse performance and accessibility, with no broken links.
